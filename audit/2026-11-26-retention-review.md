## Evaluation within the existing pilot

Read at 48 hours and 7 days after each actual publication. Record the read timestamp, age, platform, public views, engaged views, stayed/swiped percentage, average view duration, average percentage viewed, likes, shares, comments and subscribers gained where available. Record retention at the first useful beat and payoff when the curve is available. Use same-platform, similar-duration and matched-age comparisons. Mark missing metrics unavailable rather than zero. Keep the owner's pilot business targets distinct from YouTube ranking requirements. Do not delete a series automatically based on one early plateau.

If opening choice improves but retention weakens, inspect the next causal beat and caption load. If both improve without wider distribution, consider topic demand and competing content. Do not claim a universal 65%, 70% or 1,000-view threshold.
